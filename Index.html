About me

A little about me

Hi, I'm Hunter! I'm 13 and based in the South of England. I've always been fascinated by trains, from the machines themselves to the history, signalling and people behind them.

I'm keen to learn, gain new experiences and get more involved in the railway community. My portfolio documents my interests and the steps I'm taking towards those goals.
