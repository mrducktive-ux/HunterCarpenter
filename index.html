01 · About

A little about me.

Hi, I'm Hunter. I'm 13 and based in the South of England. I've been fascinated by railways for years, from the trains themselves to their history, design and operation.

Outside my interest in railways, I enjoy driving and vehicle simulation games, especially exploring how vehicles behave and how simulation can recreate real-world systems.

I'm always interested in learning new things, discovering more about engineering and transport, and building my knowledge through the hobbies I enjoy.

02 · My interests

Transport, technology & simulation.

Two sides of my interests: exploring the railway world and discovering the engineering of vehicles through games.

🚆

Railways & railway heritage

I'm fascinated by Britain's railways, including historic locomotives, modern trains, signalling, railway operations and the preservation of railway history. I enjoy learning about the people and engineering that keep the railway world moving.

My railway journey →
🚗

BeamNG.drive & vehicle physics

BeamNG.drive interests me because of its detailed soft-body vehicle physics and realistic vehicle behaviour. I enjoy the automotive side of simulation, from exploring how vehicles handle to experimenting with driving, vehicle damage and different scenarios.

Visit the official BeamNG website ↗
🕹️

Train simulation

Train simulators let me explore railway driving, routes and operations in an interactive environment. They combine my interest in trains with learning about how railway systems work.

⚙️

Engineering & technology

I'm interested in how machines work, how software recreates real-world systems and how technology can be used to create more immersive simulation experiences.

BeamNG.drive is an independent vehicle simulation game. This personal website is not affiliated with or endorsed by BeamNG GmbH.
